Keep room status accurate

Rooms & Cleaning

Make what the screen shows match the actual state of each room, so guests are never shown to an uncleaned room and no room is assigned twice.

Role: Front deskFrequency: Daily
On this page1. Check room status2. Cleaning status3. Room moves4. Room blocks5. SharingFeatures not in use

1. Check room status

Left menu → 客室の状況表 (Room status chart)
  1. Check that the date on the screen is today. According to the official manual, at properties that use daily closing, the room status chart opens on "the day after the last closed day." On mornings before closing is done, it opens on the previous day's date, so switch the date to today (dates before the previous day cannot be viewed).
  2. Check the rooms that are 「チェックイン予定」 (Arriving), 「宿泊中」 (In house), 「チェックアウト待ち」 (Awaiting check-out), 「清掃待ち」 (Awaiting cleaning) / 「清掃中」 (Cleaning), 「空室」 (Vacant), and 「ブロック中」 (Blocked). (These are the status names on the screen. For their meaning and icons, see the table in Daily tasks: "Check room status".)
  3. Look at the count for 「未部屋割りの予約」 (Unassigned reservations) in the status list on the left. Reservations without a room do not appear in the room list of the status chart. If there are 1 or more, assign them in the 「未部屋割り一覧」 (Unassigned list) of the room allocation chart (from the official manual; rooms that are candidates for assignment show a speech-bubble icon).
  4. Tell the cleaning staff about rooms that are still uncleaned with an arrival time coming up soon.
Do not judge by color aloneAlways read the room number, guest name, stay dates, and the status name shown on the screen.
Rooms with a departure and an arrival on the same day (from the official manual)For a room with a cleaning scheduled, the next arriving guest's reservation does not appear on the status chart until the cleaning is marked complete. Araki Hotel is set to create cleaning schedules automatically, so the room stays 「清掃待ち」 (Awaiting cleaning) until you set it to 「清掃済」 (Cleaned) in the cleaning schedule. If you cannot find an arriving guest on the status chart, check the cleaning schedule first (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel). No-show rooms also become 「清掃待ち」 (Awaiting cleaning) on the cleaning day. Whether a cleaning report needs 「承認」 (Approval) depends on 「請求条件」 (Billing condition) under ［清掃管理］→［手配設定］ (Cleaning management → Arrangement settings) → the property's 「詳細を見る」 (View details). If it is set to 「確認が完了しているもののみ」 (Only those confirmed), same-day arrival reservations do not appear until the cleaning report is confirmed (approved). If it is 「清掃報告されている全て」 (All reported cleanings), no approval is needed (from the official manual; Araki Hotel's billing condition setting is not yet confirmed).
Numbers at the top of the screen (from the official manual)The occupancy rate at the top of the screen is the number of rooms with a reservation that includes that day's scheduled check-in time, divided by the total number of rooms. In the status list on the left, one room can be counted under more than one status, so the counts may add up to more than the number of rooms. The size of the room tiles cannot be changed (use full-screen mode to see them larger). Not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel.
When you cannot find a reservation on the status chartIt may be a reservation with no room assigned. Search in 予約管理 → 予約情報 (Reservation management → Reservation info) (Reservation not found).

2. Check and update cleaning status

Left menu → 清掃管理 → 清掃予定表 (Cleaning management → Cleaning schedule)
  1. Check that the target date is today.
  2. Check the room numbers and cleaning schedule.
  3. Get the completion report from the cleaning staff.
  4. Read the room number back once more, then register the cleaning as complete. Press 「一括操作」 (Bulk actions) at the top right, and an action bar (未手配を全選択 (Select all unarranged) / 手配する (Arrange) / 完了する (Complete) / 確認する (Confirm)) appears at the bottom of the screen. Check the row of each room whose cleaning is done and press 「完了する」 (Complete). Without a confirmation screen, the cleaning status becomes 「清掃済」 (Cleaned), and 「［ホストにより完了］」 (Completed by host) is recorded in the 「清掃員から」 (From cleaner) column (checked 2026-10-09). Bulk actions can be used in the 「日」 (Day) and 「週」 (Week) views of the cleaning schedule (not in the month view; from the official manual). For just one room, you can also complete it by pressing that room's cleaning schedule in the cleaning schedule and using 「清掃済みに変更する」 (Change to cleaned) under 〖データ操作〗 (Data actions) (from the official manual; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  5. Go back to the room status chart and check that the rooms with arrivals are ready for guests. For a room with a departure and an arrival on the same day, after marking the cleaning complete, check that the arrival reservation appears on the status chart (see the note in Check room status).

Under ［清掃管理］ (Cleaning management) in the left menu are 「清掃予定表／組織設定／手配設定／費用単価設定／請求書発行」 (Cleaning schedule / Organization settings / Arrangement settings / Unit cost settings / Invoice issuance). The cleaning schedule shows 「物件を選択してください」 (Please select a property) until you select a property. It has a day / week / month switch and the 「新規作成」 (Create new), 「一括操作」 (Bulk actions), and 「印刷」 (Print) buttons (checked 2026-10-09).

At the top, counts are shown for room status (空室 (Vacant) / 連泊 (Multi-night) / CO予定 (Due out) / CO済 (Checked out)), cleaning status (清掃待ち (Awaiting cleaning) / 清掃中 (Cleaning) / 確認待ち (Awaiting confirmation) / 清掃済 (Cleaned)), and arrangement status (未手配 (Unarranged) / 手配済 (Arranged)). The table columns are room number/type, room status, cleaning status, stay/guests, CO/CI schedule, cleaning company/staff, cleaning notes, and to cleaner/from cleaner (checked 2026-10-09).

Cleaning schedules are created automatically when a reservation is made. Under ［清掃管理］→［手配設定］ (Cleaning management → Arrangement settings) → the property's 「詳細を見る」 (View details), 「清掃予定自動作成」 (Auto-create cleaning schedule) is set to 「チェックアウト時のみ」 (At check-out only) (the other choices are 「作成しない」 (Do not create) and 「宿泊中は毎日」 (Daily during stay)). The cleaning day is the reservation's scheduled check-out date. The 10/9 schedule showed 「清掃予定がありません」 (No cleaning scheduled) because no room was due to check out on 10/9 (checked 2026-10-09).

Cleaning during a multi-night stay is not on the scheduleAccording to the official manual, choosing 「宿泊中は毎日」 (Daily during stay) creates a cleaning schedule for each night. With Araki Hotel's 「チェックアウト時のみ」 (At check-out only), cleaning of rooms during a multi-night stay (stay cleaning) does not appear on the cleaning schedule. When needed, create it by hand with 「新規作成」 (Create new) in the cleaning schedule, or share it with the cleaning staff on paper or similar (the manager (person in charge) decides which; changing the setting is the administrator's decision).

The cleaning schedule can be switched between day / week / month, and the month view shows counts per day (unarranged, awaiting cleaning, etc.). Right after opening, it sometimes showed 「清掃予定がありません」 (No cleaning scheduled), and the list appeared after a short wait. For reservations with no room assigned, the room type (e.g. キャビン (Cabin)) is shown instead of the room number (checked 2026-10-09).

For early check-outs, also check the next day's scheduleEven if a guest checks out earlier than planned, the cleaning schedule stays on the original scheduled check-out date. Example: if a reservation due to check out on 10/10 checks out on 10/9, the cleaning appears on the 10/10 schedule as 「CO済・清掃待ち」 (Checked out, awaiting cleaning) and not on the 10/9 schedule. Check the next day's schedule too, so you do not miss cleaning rooms of guests who left early (checked 2026-10-09).
Cleaning schedules when dates change (from the official manual)When you change a reservation's check-out date (extending or shortening the stay), the automatically created cleaning schedule is re-created to match the new dates. If you only do the check-out process, as in the example above, it stays on the original scheduled date. Cleaning schedules created or moved by hand with 「新規作成」 (Create new) or 「別日にコピーする」 (Copy to another day) do not move automatically when dates change. When a reservation is canceled or deleted, all of its cleaning schedules become 「キャンセル」 (Canceled) (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel). For how to move or delete them, see Move a cleaning schedule to another day or delete it.
Arrangement and cleaner reports are awaiting confirmationNo cleaning organizations or cleaners are registered (［組織設定］ (Organization settings) is empty, and 「対応組織」 (Assigned organization) in the arrangement settings is also empty). So 「手配する」 (Arrange), 「確認する」 (Confirm), and reports from the cleaner app are not yet confirmed.
Do not mark "cleaning complete" before the reportMarking it complete only on screen can lead to guests being shown to an uncleaned room.
How to know it is doneThree things match: "the actual room," "what the cleaning staff know," and "what the PMS shows."

Bulk action buttons

The bulk actions in step 4 can be started not only with the 「一括操作」 (Bulk actions) button but also by checking a cleaning schedule's checkbox directly. In the day view the checkbox is at the left end of each row, and in the week view at the top left of each schedule. You can select all displayed schedules at once with the checkbox at the very top in the day view, or the date checkbox in the week view (from the official manual; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

Move a cleaning schedule to another day or delete it

The following is from the official manual (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel). Operate from the 〖データ操作〗 (Data actions) section in the details that open when you press a cleaning schedule.

  • Move to another day or add one: Press 「別日にコピーする」 (Copy to another day), select the date, and press 「コピーする」 (Copy) (keeps the original schedule too) or 「コピーしてこの予定を削除する」 (Copy and delete this schedule) (moves it).
  • Delete: Press 「清掃予定を削除する」 (Delete cleaning schedule). A schedule with cleaning staff assigned cannot be deleted unless you first press 「清掃担当の割り当てを解除する」 (Unassign cleaning staff). A schedule where 「清掃を開始する」 (Start cleaning) has been pressed (in cleaning) cannot be deleted.
  • How to tell an automatically created schedule: ［自動作成］ (Auto-created) appears in the date field of the details.
  • When a reservation's dates change: A schedule you deleted by hand does not come back because of the date change. A schedule in cleaning is not canceled by a date change either.
  • When a reservation is canceled or deleted: All of that reservation's cleaning schedules become 「キャンセル」 (Canceled), including those created or moved by hand and those in cleaning (this is handled differently from a date change).

Cleaning management operating patterns

The official manual organizes how cleaning management is used along three points: "share schedules as PDF (paper) or online," "managed by facility staff or by a cleaning company," and "share schedules only, also manage progress, or also keep reports with photos (evidence)," and divides them into the following patterns. If you choose online sharing, cleaners see their schedules on their own screens and can send completion reports and detailed reports.

Araki Hotel's current setupNo cleaning organizations or cleaners are registered, so operations are close to pattern A. The difference is that the front desk presses 「完了する」 (Complete) in the cleaning schedule after receiving the cleaning staff's report (based on the state of the screens checked on 2026-10-09). The manager decides whether to switch to the cleaner app (B–D).

3. A guest in house moves to another room

  1. Open the guest's reservation details and check the date of the move.
  2. Check that the destination room is vacant, cleaned, and not blocked.
  3. Change the room assignment from the move date onward.
  4. Check that the stay dates, old room, new room, and charges are as intended, then save.
  5. Exchange the keys and collect the key to the old room.
  6. Share the old room, new room, and move date with the cleaning staff and the next shift.
Use the right one of two methodsTo swap the room for the whole stay (the room was not cleaned at arrival, equipment broke, etc.), use 「ルームチェンジ」 (Room change) (Room change). To move to another room partway through a multi-night stay, use 「宿泊変更」 (Change stay) → 「部屋変更」 (Change room) to change the room from the move date onward (Changing rooms mid-stay).
Do not re-create the reservationAccording to the official manual (PDF and online versions), a room move partway through a multi-night stay is done from 「宿泊変更」 (Change stay) within the reservation. Creating a separate reservation splits the charges, guests, and payment, so do not re-create it on your own judgment.

4. Block a room

Use this to stop sales and assignment for a room because of a breakdown, repairs, in-house use, etc.

物件管理 → 部屋情報 → the room's 「詳細を見る」 → ブロック設定 (Property management → Room info → View details → Block settings)
  1. Check the room number, start date and time, and end date and time. To stop one night, make the period span 0:00 of the next day (e.g. 15:00 that day to 11:00 the next day). According to the official manual, if 0:00 of the next day falls within the block period, that day is treated as blocked and inventory is reduced.
  2. Check that there are no reservations in the same period.
  3. In 〖ブロック設定〗 (Block settings) on the room details, press 「ブロック期間を追加する」 (Add block period), select the period, and press 「OK」. Write the reason (such as a breakdown) and the planned release in the memo field (the memo is shown on the room status chart and room allocation chart in place of the guest name).
  4. Press 「変更を保存する」 (Save changes) to register it.
  5. On the room status chart, check that the period is now purple and shows 「ブロック中」 (Blocked).
  6. Leave "room, reason, period, and who decides the release" in the handover.

To release it, press the row's 「×」 in the same 〖ブロック設定〗 (Block settings), then press 「変更を保存する」 (Save changes). For detailed steps with screenshots, see Blocking a room and Release a block (checked 2026-10-07).

Blocks on closed dates cannot be changed or deletedAccording to the official manual, blocks on dates for which daily closing is done can be neither changed nor deleted. "Cannot be set for a period with reservations" is stated in the official PDF, is not in the online version, and has not been confirmed on Araki Hotel's real screen either. In either case, do not move reservations on your own or force the registration by changing the period; consult the manager.
