HOTEL SMART operations manual by scene / Initial setup

Before you start. The initial settings around property information, explained by where they are on the screen

This page covers the property details page, opened from 【物件管理】＞【物件情報】＞「詳細を見る」 (Property management > Property information > View details) in the admin screen, and the five settings screens linked from it (tablet display settings / automatic email settings / guest My Page settings / automatic room assignment settings / payment method settings). It is organized by task (scene), based on what we checked by going through the actual admin screens. It digs into the range that the official startup guide presents as "initial setup part 1 and part 2" down to the level of each item on the screen. Scenes 1 and 2 include screenshots of the actual admin screen.

1Register the property's basic information

物件管理 (Property management) ＞ 物件情報 (Property information) ＞ 「詳細を見る」 (View details) for the property name ＞ 基本情報 (Basic information) (top of the page)

This is the information that serves as the "face" of the property, such as the property name, address and check-in time. The property name can be registered separately in four languages (Japanese / English / Chinese / Korean) by switching tabs.

  1. In the 【物件管理】＞【物件情報】 (Property management > Property information) list, click 「詳細を見る」 (View details) next to the property name to open the property details page.
  2. Enter the property name, address and postal code, switching between the basic information language tabs (Japanese / English / Chinese / Korean).
  3. Enter the email address and phone number (used as the contact for guest inquiries). According to the official manual, this email address is where staff notifications are sent and is the reply-to address for emails to guests (the sender is a different address). If you change it, the destination for notifications also changes (from the official manual).
  4. Register the exterior photo from 「設定する」 (Set). (You can check the recommended size with the help icon. The official manual gives 1280×800px.)
  5. Choose how the access map is shown (do not show / specify from the address / specify latitude and longitude).
  6. Set the standard check-in and check-out times and whether early check-in and similar options are allowed.

  • Early check-in: Lets guests check in on the tablet before the reservation's check-in time. When set to 「許可する」 (Allow), choose how the time from which check-in is possible is decided: 「固定時刻」 (Fixed time) (up to 5 hours before the property's check-in time, in 15-minute steps) or 「相対時刻」 (Relative time) (30 minutes to 5 hours before the reservation's check-in time). If the previous reservation's check-out time is later, it becomes that time or later. This time is not shown to guests and is also used for the validity period of smart lock keys. The tablet shows 「チェックイン可能時間前です」 (It is before the check-in time) when it is earlier than this time (When a guest cannot check in).
  • Check-in per guest: For reservations with several guests, lets check-in go through to completion even if not all guests' information is complete. When only some guests are done, the status becomes 「一部チェックイン」 (Partially checked in).
  • Check-in per room: For properties where staff switch the status face to face. When allowed, you can check in and check out each room of a multi-room reservation separately (for steps, see Check-in per room). It was already allowed at the annex (checked 2026-10-06, from front-operations). The setting at Tanegashima Araki Hotel needs to be checked on the real screen.
Proceed while unassignedWhether to allow check-in to proceed while no room is assigned. From the official manual: even if allowed, check-in cannot be completed on the tablet alone, and at the end the guest is directed to the front desk (for staffed front desks)
Automatic check-out settingChoose when to automatically set the status to checked out, from 1 to 12 hours after the scheduled check-out time (「自動処理を行わない」 (Do not process automatically) can also be chosen)
TipThe property name and check-in/check-out times are carried straight into the guest My Page, the tablet and the merge keywords of automatic emails (shown as 「差し込みタグ」 (Merge tags) on the automatic email settings screen, e.g. [物件名（日本語）] (property name, Japanese)). Setting these correctly first makes the later settings go smoothly.

2Register room types, rooms and floors

物件管理 (Property management) ＞ 物件情報 (Property information) ＞ 詳細を見る (View details) ＞ 部屋タイプ／階層 (Room types / Floors) section (to add rooms, use 「部屋を追加する」 (Add room) at the bottom of the same property details page)

First create the room type containers such as "single" and "double", then add the actual room numbers from the property details page and link the floor information.

  1. In the 「部屋タイプ」 (Room types) section of the property details page, enter the room type name, switching between the four language tabs. According to the official manual, you cannot save unless all four language fields are filled in. Room type names currently appear only in the admin screen, so the same Japanese name in all four is fine (from the official manual).
  2. Enter the 「デフォルト宿泊人数」 (Default number of guests) and 「定員」 (Capacity) for each room type. According to the official manual, automatic room assignment judges over-capacity using each room's capacity (room information > View details). What the capacity in the room type section is used for needs to be checked on the real screen (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  3. Add a type with 「＋部屋タイプを追加する」 (+ Add room type), and delete rows you do not need with the 「×」 on the right.
  4. In the 「階層」 (Floors) section, register the building's floor information (floor names) from 「＋階層を追加する」 (+ Add floor); you can then assign a floor to each room. Choose each room's floor in 「階数」 (Floor) in the room information details (from the official manual; for its use, see the tip in scene 3).
  5. Register individual room numbers from 「部屋を追加する」 (Add room) at the bottom of the property details page. 【物件管理】＞【部屋情報】 (Property management > Room information) is the screen for viewing and filtering registered rooms.
Main fields in the room details (room information > View details) (from the official manual; limited to the fields Araki Hotel uses)
Room numberRoom number or room name
Room typeChoose from the dropdown. Automatic room assignment is based on this room type. If you change the room type, the number of rooms per room type on the property details page also changes
CapacityNumber of people, including children. You can save with it blank, but reservations over the capacity are excluded from automatic room assignment
FloorChoose from the floors created in 「階層」 (Floors) on the property details page. Not set at Araki Hotel (checked 2026-10-09; see the tip in scene 3)
Key number / room entry instructionsThe key number, which can be given on the check-in completion screen or by email, and room entry instructions per language (the merge keyword ［鍵番号］ (key number) can be used). How Araki Hotel uses these is not confirmed
Block settingUsed to stop selling a room because of a fault, repairs and so on (Block a room)
CautionRenaming a room type later also affects how it is shown in its links to existing reservations, charge settings and rate plans. We recommend finalizing the names before you start operating.

Arrange the rooms by building floor so that the status of every room can be seen at a glance on the room status chart. Newly registered rooms are just lined up in one row under 「未配置の部屋」 (Unplaced rooms), so always place them after registering.

  2. Click 「レイアウト変更」 (Change layout) at the bottom right of the screen. A confirmation 「部屋の配置を変更しますか？」 (Change the room layout?) appears; click OK to enter edit mode (⊕ buttons appear above, below, left and right of the layout grid).
  3. Drag and drop rooms from 「未配置の部屋」 (Unplaced rooms) to their positions according to the placement rules. If there are not enough cells (rows/columns), add them with the ⊕ buttons above, below, left and right of the layout grid.

Caution
  • If you move to another screen without clicking 「レイアウト変更」 (Change layout), the layout is not saved.
  • When you save, rows and columns at the edges with no rooms in them are removed automatically.
  • Newly registered rooms appear under 「未配置の部屋」 (Unplaced rooms), so place them with the same steps according to the placement rules.
  • Rows with too many rooms to fit on the screen (such as the 24 cabins) are shown with horizontal scrolling. To see the whole chart, use 「縮小表示」 (Compact view) at the top of the screen or full-screen view.

It seems to be the field used for filtering by 「フロア」 (Floor) on the room allocation chart, so consider setting it if needed (needs checking).
According to the official manual, the floor is used for 「フロア一括割当」 (Floor bulk assignment) (a feature that reserves all rooms on a chosen floor at once when creating a new reservation; for groups such as school trips) (not checked on the real screen). If you take groups, consider setting it (Floor bulk assignment). The official manual does not say whether it affects the 「フロア」 (Floor) filter on the room allocation chart, so this still needs to be checked on the real screen.

4Set up the meal menu (meal items)

物件管理 (Property management) ＞ 物件情報 (Property information) ＞ 詳細を見る (View details) ＞ 食事設定／食事項目 (Meal settings / Meal items) section

These settings are for properties that serve meals such as breakfast and dinner. You can have meals reflected automatically in rate plans at booking, and manage meal locations and serving times.

Not currently used at Araki HotelWe do not serve meals at present, so do not change these settings. If we start serving meals in the future, set them up after checking with the manager (person in charge) and HOTEL SMART.
Meal settings: fields
Breakfast charge posting dateChoose "serving day" or "previous day". Affects the tally date in sales reports
Meal itemsFrom 「＋食事項目を追加する」 (+ Add meal item), register each meal item's name / category / charge item / unit price / meal location / serving time
TipMeal items registered here are reflected in 「お食事」 (Meals) in the 〖予約情報〗 (Reservation information) section of the reservation creation screen, and in the meal status chart, meal location reservation list and summary table under 【食事管理】 (Meal management). Properties with several meal locations will find it smoother to set up 「食事場所一覧」 (Meal location list) (in the meal management menu) first and then link them here.

7Register facilities and arranged items

物件管理 (Property management) ＞ 物件情報 (Property information) ＞ 詳細を見る (View details) ＞ 付帯設備／手配品 (Facilities / Arranged items) section

Register "facility" booking slots such as meeting rooms, parking and rental bicycles, and "arranged items" such as amenities and rental goods, each separately.

FacilitiesRegister the facility name and capacity from 「＋付帯施設を追加する」 (+ Add facility). Used on the 【付帯設備管理】＞【設備利用状況】 (Facility management > Facility usage) screen, which manages usage on a timeline. To use it, set 「付帯設備予約」 (Facility reservations) to 「表示する」 (Show) in reservation detail display settings (from the official manual). In the official manual the button is called 「付帯設備を追加する」 (Add facility) (the wording on the real screen needs checking)
Arranged itemsRegister the item name, display order, default quantity and enabled/disabled from 「＋手配品を追加する」 (+ Add arranged item). Reflected in reservation management > arranged item list. An item that has been used in a reservation even once cannot be deleted, so when you stop using it, uncheck 「有効」 (Enabled) (from the official manual)
TipSetting a "default quantity" for arranged items saves you entering the quantity when creating a new reservation.
From the official manual: enter the display order as an integer of 0 or more and the default quantity as an integer of 1 or more (otherwise it cannot be saved). Even if you uncheck 「有効」 (Enabled), arranged items already added to reservations stay as they are.
How they are used after registration (from the official manual, not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel)
  • Facilities: Use them in 〖付帯設備予約〗 (Facility reservations) in reservation details by entering the facility, usage period and number of people. A warning appears when the number of people exceeds the capacity or the time overlaps another reservation (overbooking). Facility charges are not entered automatically, so register them separately in the charge information. Usage can be seen on a timeline in 【付帯設備管理】＞【設備利用状況】 (Facility management > Facility usage).
  • Facility-only reservations: Register reservations that use no room with 「0部屋の予約にする」 (Make it a 0-room reservation) so they do not affect occupancy and similar figures. When such a reservation exists, daily closing shows 「未チェックインの予約があります」 (There are reservations not checked in), but the official manual says you can close as is.
  • Arranged items: Specify the room, arrangement date and quantity in 〖手配品情報〗 (Arranged item information) in reservation details, and check them together by day in 【予約管理】＞【手配品一覧】 (Reservation management > Arranged item list). When you do a room change or move dates, the arranged items move along with it.
  • For operations on the reservation side, see Front desk operations.
CautionAccording to the official manual, once a reservation is canceled its facility information is deleted, and it does not come back even if the cancellation is undone (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

10Set up the check-in tablet

This section builds the tablet screens that guests actually touch. It has more items than any other part of the initial setup. Work from top to bottom and you will not get lost.

① Decide the identity verification method (page settings)

らくらくチェックイン (Easy check-in)A mode that skips part of identity verification and form entry to shorten check-in. From the official manual: when set to 「行う」 (Use), three things are skipped: (1) language selection, so the tablet shows Japanese only (language selection remains on a tablet set up for more than one property); (2) the tutorial; (3) guest information entry, but only when every guest has already entered all required items. It is described as meant for staffed hotels with mostly Japanese guests
タブレットによるチェックアウト (Check-out by tablet)Whether to offer self check-out on the tablet. From the official manual: setting it to 「行う」 (Use) does not force guests to use the tablet. When set to 「行わない」 (Do not use), the check-in/check-out selection screen that follows language selection no longer appears, and check-out is handled by automatic check-out (Scene 1), the check-out button on guest My Page (Scene 12), or switching the status at the front desk
本人確認方法 (Identity verification method)Choose from 行わない (None) / 確認動画撮影 (Record verification video) / 内部ビデオチャット (Internal video chat). From the official manual: check with the public health center how much identity verification is needed. If you verify by taking a face photo, choose 「行わない」 (None) here. The call center only checks the name and, for foreign nationals, whether the passport belongs to the guest; it cannot handle anything else or record
確認動画撮影秒数 (Verification video length)「5秒×宿泊者数」 (5 seconds × number of guests) or 「3秒」 (3 seconds). From the official manual: 「5秒×宿泊者数」 is based on the number of guests in the reservation; for a 5-person reservation it records 25 seconds no matter how many people actually arrive. When 「リアルタイム確認」 (Real-time check) is set to 「行う」 (Use), the tablet shows a waiting screen until staff approve on the admin screen, and after the set number of minutes it automatically moves on to check-in complete (how to respond: ⑥)
本人確認タイミング (Identity verification timing)When verification takes place, for example after the passport is captured. サポート通話 (Support call)Turns on a feature that lets guests make a video call from the tablet to the property. From the official manual: the call goes to property staff, not to the call center. On each PC that receives calls, set this per browser: icon at the top right ＞ computer icon ＞ 「ビデオチャット受信物件」 (Video chat receiving property) (the same applies to internal video chat and real-time video checks). Calls are hard to receive on an iPhone or iPad, so a PC is recommended (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel)
無操作でトップに戻る秒数 (Seconds of inactivity before returning to the top)The number of seconds without any input before the screen resets. The official manual describes this as a setting to keep guests' personal information from staying on the screen

② Check-in code settings

チェックインコード入力欄（4言語） (Check-in code entry field, 4 languages)The property name in several languages, shown on the tablet (the official manual explains that this screen is where guests choose how to enter, with three options: 6-digit check-in code / QR code / check-in without a code. That does not match "property name in several languages", so check on the real screen what this field is for)
チェックインコード不要チェックイン/チェックアウト (Check-in/check-out without a check-in code)Whether guests can skip entering the code. ④ Guest information entry

For many items, such as name in kana, prefecture, nationality, occupation, date of birth, gender, previous place of stay / next destination, passport number, face photo, ID document, thermometer, vaccination certificate and negative test certificate, you can tick 「必須」 (Required) and 「代表者のみ」 (Lead guest only). You can also use 「＋項目を追加する」 (+ Add item) to add entry patterns for each language/nationality segment. Site controller: TL-Lincoln (two-way integration)

Araki Hotel's site controller is TL-Lincoln, connected two-way. With two-way, in addition to importing reservations from TL-Lincoln (1way), inventory reduced by reservations and blocks created in HOTEL SMART is sent back to TL-Lincoln. For this reason, inventory (the number of rooms put on sale) is managed as HOTEL SMART's sales inventory, and the number of remaining rooms is not changed directly in the TL-Lincoln admin screen. The mechanism and settings below are as explained in the official manual (Araki Hotel's settings screen is not yet confirmed).

  • The inventory sent to TL-Lincoln is calculated as "rooms − reservations − blocks + adjustment", and any change is sent automatically. It can take up to about 15 minutes to be reflected.
  • Every time you add, change or cancel a reservation, set a block or change the adjustment, the inventory is sent. You do not need to fix the inventory on the TL-Lincoln side by hand after changing dates.
  • Even if you change the number of remaining rooms by hand in TL-Lincoln, it is overwritten with HOTEL SMART's number at the next send. Inventory cannot be sent to allotments set to 「照会のみ」 (Inquiry only) on the TL-Lincoln side.
  • TL-Lincoln's inventory is split into two streams: 「ネット販売」 (Online sales) and 「旅行会社」 (Travel agencies). Online sales are managed per 「ネット室タイプグループ」 (online room type group), and booking sites in the same group share the remaining rooms (inventory cannot be split by site). Travel agencies are managed per "sales destination code × travel agency room type × allotment". To hold rooms for each travel agency, use a separate feature called allotment integration.
  1. On the 【TL-リンカーン連携】 (TL-Lincoln integration) screen, register the user ID and password to start the 1way integration, then run 「部屋タイプマスター取得」 (Get room type master) and 「予約情報取得」 (Get reservation data) in 〖アカウント情報〗 (Account information) on the right.
  2. Complete the 「販売在庫」 (Sales inventory) settings below (sales room types and sales inventory calendar).
  3. In 【TL-リンカーン連携】＞「詳細を見る」＞【アカウント設定】 (TL-Lincoln integration > View details > Account settings), under 〖アカウント情報〗 (Account information), press 「開始」 (Start) for ［販売在庫連携］ (Sales inventory integration) (if there is no button, ask support to enable it). Inventory for the period created in the sales inventory calendar is synced from today up to the end of the same month of the following year at most.
  4. On the same screen, in 〖在庫連携設定（ネット販売）〗 (Inventory integration settings (online sales)) for each online room type group, and in 〖在庫連携設定（旅行会社）〗 (Inventory integration settings (travel agencies)) for each sales destination code (allotment), choose ［割当て先販売在庫］ (Target sales inventory) and press 「変更を保存する」 (Save changes). If a room type on sale has no target, inventory is not sent correctly.
Caution (from the official manual)Two-way can be stopped with the 「停止」 (Stop) button of ［販売在庫連携］ (Sales inventory integration), but if you stop it, inventory is no longer sent to TL-Lincoln. Consult support before stopping it. At Araki Hotel, do not operate it without the manager's decision.

Sales inventory (sales room types and sales inventory calendar)

販売管理 ＞ 販売部屋タイプ／販売在庫 ＞ 販売在庫カレンダー (Sales management > Sales room types / Sales inventory > Sales inventory calendar)

The screen for managing the inventory numbers reported to the site controller through the two-way integration. The following is as explained in the official manual (Araki Hotel's settings are not yet confirmed).

  1. In 【販売部屋タイプ】 (Sales room types), choose the property, then from 「項目を追加する」 (Add item) enter the sales room type name, the initial adjustment value and the room types to link, and save. Matching the names and order to the TL-Lincoln side makes them easier to follow. If you link several room types, inventory is used from the room type at the top first.
  2. In 【販売在庫カレンダー】 (Sales inventory calendar), choose the property and period and press 「在庫数を表示する」 (Show inventory).   3. Click an inventory number to open the breakdown, where you can change the adjustment or close out (stop sales). Using close-out requires an application to support. Adjustments within a period can be changed together with 「調整数一括変換」 (Bulk change adjustment).

17Set up rate plans and plan charges

プラン設定 ＞ プラン情報／プランコード設定／料金自動仕訳設定 (Plan settings > Plan information / Plan code settings / Automatic charge allocation settings), 販売管理 ＞ 料金ランク・料金グループ・料金カレンダー (Sales management > Rate ranks, rate groups, rate calendar)

Settings for telling apart the rate plan name, perks and meals attached to a reservation, and for entering plan charges automatically for phone reservations. If you don't use them, reservations can still be created and imported without these settings. The following is as explained in the official manual (the status of Araki Hotel's plan settings is not yet confirmed).

Before connecting the site controllerFinish creating rate plans and linking plan codes before starting the site controller integration. A rate plan is not attached afterwards to reservations already imported (from the official manual).

Create a rate plan

  1. In 【プラン設定】＞【プラン情報】 (Plan settings > Plan information), press 「新規プラン作成」 (Create new plan) and choose the property.
  2. Enter the plan name (required) and, if needed, the display name (a short name), plan description (for management, not shown to guests) and perk memo (shown in the reservation details).
  3. For plans with meals, check the meal category and choose the meal items (Araki Hotel currently serves no meals. Scene 4).
  4. Set the room types to sell and the rate group (can be chosen after saving once), the minimum and maximum number of nights, the check-in / check-out times and the party type. The number of nights and check-in / check-out times are used only when creating a reservation by hand, and the times take priority over the property settings.
  5. Press 「保存する」 (Save) at the top right. If there are several properties, you can create a plan by copying another property's plan.
  • Rate plans you create cannot be deleted, and they stay in the order they were created. Rate plan names in the PMS do not have to match those on the booking sites.

Link plan codes

Rate plans on booking sites and travel agencies have an alphanumeric "plan code", which arrives with the reservation through the site controller. If you link this code to a HOTEL SMART rate plan, the plan is attached to incoming reservations. You can link several codes to one plan, so plans with different names on each site can be combined into one.

  1. In 【プラン設定】＞【プランコード設定】 (Plan settings > Plan code settings), choose the property and rate plan.
  2. Enter the plan code and the plan name in the site controller (booking site), and save. If the code differs by even one character it will not link, so copy and paste it. The plan name works even if it differs slightly, but copying it as is makes it easier to tell apart.
When a reservation's plan shows 「未選択」 (Not selected)The reservation's plan code is not linked to any rate plan. Look up the code in the site controller (TL-Lincoln at Araki Hotel) or the booking site, and add it in plan code settings (from the official manual; how to look it up in TL-Lincoln is not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

Plan charges and automatic charge allocation

Reservations coming in from the site controller are imported with their charges, so this setting is not needed for them. Set it up if you want charges calculated automatically when a rate plan is chosen for a reservation created by hand, such as a phone or walk-in reservation.

  1. 料金ランク (Rate ranks): Create price-band categories for each day, such as peak / regular / off-season, or the day before a holiday and other days. Even if the charge is flat, one rank is needed for automatic calculation.
  2. 料金グループ (Rate groups): Group the charges for each rank by unit, such as rate plan × room type.
  3. 料金カレンダー (Rate calendar): Decide which rank applies to each day.
  4. プランへの適用 (Applying to the plan): Choose the rate group in the plan's room type settings.

With automatic charge allocation settings (【プラン設定】＞【料金自動仕訳設定】 (Plan settings > Automatic charge allocation settings)), you can automatically split part of the room charge into another charge item (example: 1,000 yen of a plan with a QUO Card goes to the QUO Card item). Set the charge item, unit price including tax, per person or per room, and per night or once. It cannot be split between adults and children. Meal charges are allocated in the plan's meal settings, so they are not set here.

Login security

  • Be careful not to mistype the password: if login fails many times in a short time, that connection source (the hotel's network) is automatically locked out for a while, and you cannot log in even with the correct password. Wait a while, and if it is urgent, ask support to lift it (from the official manual).
  • Passkeys: a mechanism that adds identity verification with fingerprint / face recognition or a security key. The administrator chooses the policy in ［認証器設定］ (Authenticator settings) from 「無効／有効／必須／承認必須」 (Disabled / Enabled / Required / Approval required). Even with "Enabled", once a person has registered, identity verification is required at every login after that. Passkeys are per device, so register again on a different PC. Registering several in case of loss is recommended. Fingerprint and face data are not sent to the server (from the official manual; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  • Restricting connection sources: if someone logs in from overseas or from a connection source whose location cannot be determined, a warning appears in the admin screen's notices and it is recorded. People who have registered a passkey are asked for additional identity verification. Ask support to set allow / deny rules by IP address or country (from the official manual; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  • Single sign-on (SSO) using a company identity platform (SAML) is also available, but whether Araki Hotel uses it is not yet confirmed.

19Switching from another company's system

Key points of the official manual's recommended steps toward the day you switch from the previous system to HOTEL SMART (switchover date). Araki Hotel's actual switchover steps are not yet confirmed, so follow the manager's plan.

Which system to enter reservations in (from the official manual)
Reservations checking out before the switchover dateProcess them in the previous system; do not enter them in HOTEL SMART
Reservations checking in on or after the switchover dateEnter them in HOTEL SMART. For reservations through the site controller, resend the check-ins on or after the switchover date from the site controller side and import them. Enter reservations that do not go through the site controller, such as phone reservations, by hand
Multi-night stays spanning the switchover dateEnter them in HOTEL SMART by hand, with the switchover date as the check-in date
  • Past reservations are not moved to HOTEL SMART; view them in the previous system. For new reservations that come in during the preparation period too, stays before the switchover date go in the previous system and stays from then on go in HOTEL SMART.
  • Move accounts receivable and advance payment balances to HOTEL SMART by the daily closing of the switchover date.
  • When entering by hand reservations prepaid on Rakuten Travel, Jalan and so on that span the switchover date, check that the receivable is not duplicated with the previous system.
  • Just before switching, note the number of remaining rooms for each day and room type, and after importing, check that the remaining rooms in HOTEL SMART match the inventory in the site controller (TL-Lincoln) (Sales inventory).
