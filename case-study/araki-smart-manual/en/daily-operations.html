Daily basics

Daily Front Desk Work

This page helps you avoid missed checks by working in the order "start of shift → arrivals → departures → handover."

Role: Front deskFrequency: Daily
On this page1. Start of shift2. Dashboard3. Room status4. Check-in5. Check-out6. Handover7. Daily closing8. Reports & CSV

1. First things to do when your shift starts

Screen to open: Dashboard
  • Logged in with your own account
  • Read the handover from the previous shift
  • Checked today's reservation and check-in counts in the 「チェックイン状況」 (Check-in status) and 「予約状況」 (Reservation status) tables
  • Checked the counts for 「未(昨日以前)」 (Not yet (yesterday or earlier)), 「未部屋割り」 (Unassigned) and 「オーバーブッキング」 (Overbooking), and the guests with unpaid amounts or who need attention
  • Looked at 「清掃状況(本日)」 (Cleaning status (today)) and checked for room changes or arrival time changes to pass on to the cleaning staff
When you are doneYou are done when you can explain out loud "who is arriving today, who is leaving, and what to watch out for."

2. How to read the dashboard

This is the first screen after you log in. Check the overall picture for today before editing any reservation details. The dashboard at Tanegashima Araki Hotel is laid out as follows (checked 2026-10-10).

Top row
This month's booked sales / This month's actual sales / Sales target and achievement rate
Middle row
Occupancy rate / Reservations with repeat status not yet judged / Reservations received (today, this month) / Check-ins (today, this month)
Bottom row
Check-in status (not yet (today), done (today), not yet (yesterday or earlier)) / Cleaning status (today) (scheduled, not arranged, not completed, reports awaiting review)
Reservation status table (at the very bottom)
Rooms / Today's reservations / Today's cleaning, not completed / Tomorrow's reservations / Tomorrow's cleaning, not arranged / Unassigned / Automatic room assignment failed / Overbooking. Click a number to go to the matching list.

Every morning the front desk looks at 「チェックイン状況」 (Check-in status), 「清掃状況(本日)」 (Cleaning status (today)) and the 「予約状況」 (Reservation status) table. 「未(昨日以前)」 (Not yet (yesterday or earlier)) means missed check-ins, and 「未部屋割り」 (Unassigned) and 「オーバーブッキング」 (Overbooking) need action today. Unassigned reservations are assigned a room before arrival (checked 2026-10-10).

4. Check in

客室の状況表 (Room status chart) → 当日分の予約検索 (Search today's reservations) → open the room
  1. Enter the guest's name in ［当日分の予約検索］ (Search today's reservations) at the top of the room status chart to find the reservation (room). The matching room is highlighted. You can also use 「スマート予約検索」 (Smart reservation search) at the very top of the screen to search all reservations, including those not for today (separate multiple conditions with spaces; 「過去分も表示」 (Show past ones too) can also be used).
  1. Read through the stay dates, number of guests, room type and notes together with the guest.
  2. Check the items the property requires, such as guest information and ID documents. Also check whether the room shows the 「宿泊台帳未入力」 (Guest register not filled in) or 「要領収書」 (Receipt required) icon (from the official manual. When guest information has been entered on My Page, 「マイページ入力済み」 (Entered on My Page) appears above the lead guest's name in the details. Not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  3. Check the room number and that cleaning is complete. If the reservation is unassigned, assign a room according to the manager's rules.
  1. Check whether an outstanding balance is shown in red (whether the room shows the 「未収金あり（￥）」 (Outstanding balance (￥)) icon), and take any payment needed. According to the official manual, a warning pop-up appears if you try to check in while an outstanding balance remains (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  2. Run 「チェックイン」 (Check in) and confirm that the screen shows 「宿泊中」 (In house).
  1. Hand over the key and the hotel information.
When to stopIf you cannot find the reservation, the stay dates or number of guests are different, the room has not been cleaned, or the amount to pay differs from the reservation confirmation, do not change anything based on guesses. Check "Troubleshooting" and contact the manager.
When you are doneThe reservation status is 「宿泊中」 (In house) (「チェックイン済み（宿泊中）」 (Checked in (in house)) in the reservation details), and the room number, payment and key handover all match.

Tablet self check-in

Araki Hotel uses the check-in tablet (らくらくチェックイン (Easy check-in), verification video, support call, etc.) (owner's decision, 2026-10-11). The settings for what the tablet shows are in Set up the check-in tablet (administrator). According to the official manual, the guest's steps are as follows (the screen order and wording are not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

  1. Choose a language, then choose check-in.
  2. Enter the 6-digit check-in code received by email or on My Page, or hold up the QR code (depending on the settings, the reservation can also be found without a code, by name or phone number, number of guests and check-out date).
  3. Read the information and agree to the terms and conditions of accommodation and other terms.
  4. Enter the guest information. Items already entered on My Page can be skipped.
  5. Verify identity (record a verification video, etc.).
  6. The completion screen shows the room number and other information.
What the front desk should know (from the official manual)
  • When a guest presses 「サポート通話」 (Support call) on the tablet, a video call connects to the property's staff (the settings for the PC that receives it are in Decide the identity verification method).
  • For passports and face photos, recording and registration cannot finish unless a face is visible (other documents are not checked).
  • For a multi-guest reservation where guests arrive at different times, if the property settings allow 「宿泊者別チェックイン」 (Check-in per guest), guests who arrive later can enter the same code and do their own check-in.
  • Payment on the tablet cannot be split. Guests who want to split payment pay at the front desk.
  • How to handle errors shown on the tablet (such as 「チェックインコードが不正です」 (The check-in code is invalid)) is covered in Troubleshooting.
Not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel.
Call center identity verification (paid option, from the official manual)A service for unstaffed hours in which an operator verifies the guest's identity by video call. It runs 24 hours a day in three languages (Japanese, English and Chinese) and checks the name, number of guests and number of nights, and for foreign nationals, that the face matches the passport photo. It only verifies identity; the operators do not take help calls (support calls). After entering their information, the guest chooses a language, starts the call, answers the questions, and presses 「次へ進む」 (Next) when verification is done. Because the system cannot tell whether the call has ended, 「次へ進む」 (Next) becomes available after the set wait time (30–180 seconds, default 30 seconds). If it is set too short, guests can move on without a call. If audio or video does not come through, go through check-in with a test reservation and check the camera and microphone from the gear icon on the call screen. Whether Araki Hotel has subscribed to this option is not yet confirmed.

According to the official manual, self check-in guide materials for guests (for posting in the hotel and for attaching to emails) are provided and can be edited to fit the property (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

5. Check out

客室の状況表 (Room status chart) → 当日分の予約検索 (Search today's reservations) → open the room
  1. On the room status chart, open the room by the guest's name (［当日分の予約検索］ (Search today's reservations)) or by room number.
  2. Check the room charge, additional charges and the amount already paid.
  3. If anything is unpaid, confirm the payment method and take payment.
  4. Check whether a receipt is needed, and the addressee and description. For amounts prepaid on a booking site, the hotel does not issue a receipt; direct the guest to the booking site's receipt (from the official manual. Issue a receipt).
  5. Confirm that the key has been returned and press 「チェックアウト」 (Check out) in the room details. If the 「チェックアウト」 (Check out) button does not appear (for example, when the status chart's date is not yet the check-out date; checked 2026-10-05), choose 「チェックアウト済みにする」 (Mark as checked out) from the status at the top right of the reservation details, select the rooms to check out, and run it.
  1. Confirm that the status has changed to 「チェックアウト済み」 (Checked out), and let the cleaning staff know.
Check additional usage firstIf there is food and drink, shop purchases, an extension or similar, add it to the statement before pressing check-out.

6. Hand over to the next shift

Do not assume "it's obvious if you look." Leave notes so the next person can act without hesitation.

  • Guests who have not arrived or are arriving late, and the contact status
  • Unpaid amounts, amount discrepancies, refunds pending
  • Room moves, breakdowns, blocks, rooms awaiting cleaning
  • Items lent out, items arranged, special handling
  • Requests from guests, and who does what next
ExampleWrite the subject, the content, what has been done and what remains, for example: "Room 305, Mr./Ms. Tanaka / extension cord on loan / to be returned at check-out / next person on duty confirms the return."

7. Daily closing (manager)

Administrators and the manager only

This process finalizes the sales and payments for the business day. If the property has enabled the feature, the 「日次の締めを行う」 (Run daily closing) button appears on the daily closing screen after the set closing time, and the manager checks everything and then presses it to close the day.

Only an administrator enables it the first timeAfter deciding Araki Hotel's policy for using it, who is responsible, the closing time and how to reconcile, open 【売上管理】→【日次締め機能】 (Sales management → Daily closing feature), check the target property and closing time, and run 「日次締め機能を有効化する」 (Enable the daily closing feature). Regular front desk staff do not set this. Once enabled, the daily closing feature cannot be disabled from the admin screen. Also, when it was enabled after that day's closing time had already passed, the days up to the previous day were closed on the spot (checked 2026-10-06). Reservations, charges and payments on closed days can no longer be changed, so before enabling it, check that no reservations or payments that need fixing remain.

At Tanegashima Araki Annex, once the closing time (04:00 every day) passes, the 「日次の締めを行う」 (Run daily closing) button appears at the very bottom of the daily closing screen. The day is not closed automatically even after the closing time (checked 2026-10-07). After the closing time, the manager closes the day with the following steps.

A day runs from 04:00 to 03:59 the next day (from the official manual)According to the official manual, the 24 hours from the closing time make up one day for that property. Araki Hotel's closing time is 04:00, so payments and charges entered between midnight and 4:00 are treated as part of the previous day (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
  1. Open 【売上管理】→【日次締め機能】 (Sales management → Daily closing feature), press 「再取得」 (Reload) to get the latest state, and check the target date (a date not yet closed), the reservations with notices, the payment information and the advance payment information.
  1. Check that no check-ins or check-outs were missed on the target date (the day being closed).
  2. Check unpaid amounts and discrepancies by payment type, such as cash and card.
  3. Check that no reservations or statement items are left unhandled.
  4. If there are discrepancies or unprocessed items, handle them before closing.
  5. If there are no problems, press 「日次の締めを行う」 (Run daily closing). In the confirmation 「（物件名）の（日付）分の売上・売掛金を締めますか？」 (Close sales and accounts receivable for (property name) for (date)?), check the property name and date, then press OK (checked 2026-10-07).
Closing does not stop even if reservations with notices remainAccording to the official manual, even if notices (errors) are shown, pressing 「OK」 on the confirmation screen continues the closing, and when there are errors the confirmation screen appears a second time. If a second confirmation screen appears, it means unhandled reservations remain. Go back without pressing OK, handle them, and then close again (the wording of the second confirmation screen is not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
Data cannot be edited after closingThe official front operations manual says that data from closed days cannot be edited. Because nothing can be changed after closing, handle discrepancies and unprocessed items before pressing 「日次の締めを行う」 (Run daily closing).

8. Output reports and CSVs (manager)

For administrators and the manager
分析帳票 (Analysis reports) → 帳票・CSV出力 (Report/CSV output) (for the income and expenditure report: 売上管理 (Sales management) → 収支実績報告書 (Income and expenditure results report))

These reports are used to check daily sales and payments and to output guest lists. The following summarizes the official manual's explanations; the output results are not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel. Every report is output per property, so output the main building and the annex separately.

Terms to know first (from the official manual)"Charges" are the amounts billed to the guest, and "payments" are the amounts actually received. Sales count on each charge's "usage date," and charges with no usage date count on the reservation's check-out date. Amounts paid with points are included in sales. The monthly totals in reports are updated once a day, and 「集計日時」 (Aggregation date/time) at the top right shows when they were totaled.

Occupancy output (occupancy and sales for one day)

  • In 〖分析〗 (Analysis), choose the property and date under 「稼働出力」 (Occupancy output) and press 「出力する」 (Output); one Excel file is saved. If you press it without choosing a property, nothing is output.
  • It shows the rooms and capacity available for sale (excluding rooms taken out of service), rooms occupied and total guests, the number of rooms for same-day bookings, no-shows and same-day cancellations, sales for the day and month-to-date (including and excluding tax), a comparison with the same day last year, and a breakdown of sales other than room charges.
  • If you choose the last day of the month, the month-to-date total is the total for that month. The daily closing time (04:00 at Araki Hotel) is the boundary of a day.
  • Reservations canceled after the target date are still included in that day's results. Non-reservation charges are added in, and items for point use, tax, charges not counted as sales and partial payments are excluded.

Payment information CSV and payment information (breakdown) CSV

  • Output it from 「精算情報」 (Payment information) in 〖会計〗 (Accounting), choosing the property and period (up to 1 month). With a row for each payment method and a column for each date, it lists the total of the payments whose payment date is that day (with refunds subtracted). The top row is 「支払い方法未入力」 (Payment method not entered).
  • 「精算情報（内訳）」 (Payment information (breakdown)) is output for one chosen day and lists each payment whose payment date is that day on its own row (registration date and time, check-in code, room number, amount, payment method, accounts receivable party, remarks, etc.). For non-reservation charges and payments, the check-in code and lead guest name are blank.
  • Both include reservation and non-reservation items. The date in the file name of the period total is the output date (for properties using daily closing, the closing date).
  • Payment methods set to 「表示しない」 (Do not display) are left out entirely if there were no payments with them in the period, and appear if there were.
  • No report filters payments by payment date, so the breakdown of OTA (online travel agency) prepayments cannot be seen in this report.

Advance payment and in-house receivable balance list CSV

  • Shows, for each reservation as of the chosen date, the balance of advance payments (amounts received in advance) and in-house receivables (amounts not yet received). Choose the property and date (up to today) to output it. It can be output only for properties using daily closing.
  • One row per reservation (one per registration for non-reservation items), and reservations with neither balance are not output. For non-reservation rows, the check-in code column shows 「（予約外）」 (Non-reservation) and the date columns are empty.
  • Charges are counted by usage date (if there is none, the check-out date for reservations and the registration date for non-reservation items), and charges minus payments decides whether it is an advance payment or a receivable. Canceled reservations are not included.

In-house receivables CSV

  • Output it from 「滞在未収金」 (In-house receivables) in 〖会計〗 (Accounting), choosing the property and target date (today or earlier). It is the report version of the in-stay payment checklist.
  • Each room in house (checked in) on the target date is listed on its own row, with the total outstanding balance excluding and including tax. Rooms with a 0-yen balance are also listed. Reservations not yet checked in and reservations already checked out are not listed.
  • Charges dated after the target date, charges not counted as sales, and items linked to partial payments or specified-amount payments are not counted as outstanding.

Charges/payments update history CSV (operation history)

  • Shows when and by whom charges and payments were created, changed or deleted. It is output per property, for a period of up to 3 months.
  • If you specify a reservation by check-in code or OTA reservation number, all of that reservation's history is output regardless of the operation date (non-reservation slips are not included). You can filter by charges or payments, and by new, deleted, before change and after change.
  • A change is recorded as two rows, "before change" and "after change." The operator column (column D) shows who made the operation, such as staff, tablet or site controller, and if separate accounts are used, the account name appears.

Guest register (the register required by the Hotel Business Act)

  • Output it from 「宿泊者台帳」 (Guest register) in 〖旅館業法対応_帳票〗 (Hotel Business Act reports), choosing the property, period and condition. It cannot be output if there are more than 5000 records, so split the period.
  • Choose the condition from 「チェックイン」 (Check-in) (default), 「利用日」 (Usage date) or 「チェックアウト」 (Check-out). 「利用日」 (Usage date) covers reservations staying during the period, excluding the check-out day and day use (example: arriving 7/8 and leaving 7/10 covers 7/8 and 7/9).
  • It shows the scheduled and actual check-in and check-out times, nationality, name, address, occupation, passport number, previous place of stay, next destination, and more.

Reservation CSV (per reservation / per room)

  • Both cover reservations whose check-in date is within the period. 「予約単位」 (Per reservation) has one row per reservation, and 「部屋単位」 (Per room) has one row per room per night (2 rooms for 3 nights gives 6 rows).
  • They show nationality, breakdown of guests, booking channel, amounts by charge item and by payment method, prepaid and pay-at-the-hotel amounts, requests, operator memos, and more (per room also includes OTA commissions). Amounts are, as a rule, excluding tax.
  • If you set the per-reservation period to today only, you can use it as a list of reservations checking in today.
  • You cannot output only cancellations. Output with 「キャンセルを含む」 (Include cancellations), then look at the canceled rows.

Guest information list

  • 予約管理 → 宿泊者情報 (Reservation management → Guest information) lists name, property, check-in code, booking channel, dates and contact information, and 「詳細を見る」 (View details) opens the guest details. You can also open them by clicking the name in 〖宿泊者情報〗 (Guest information) in the reservation details. Content entered on My Page can also be seen here (past reservations too).
  • After correcting the guest details, press 「変更を保存する」 (Save changes). Only the guest can enter the signature and the form memo.
  • You cannot filter by age group or similar, so if needed, output a CSV and process it. Companion information can be deleted from the reservation details, and the lead guest's information is deleted when the reservation is deleted. The manual says the data remains for the contract period and that registered images cannot be deleted (replace them with a blank image).

Income and expenditure results report

  • In 売上管理 → 収支実績報告書 (Sales management → Income and expenditure results report), choose a property (one at a time) and a period (up to 3 months) to see sales, expenses, payments and advance payments received. Only properties using daily closing are totaled.
  • Totals are calculated automatically after closing. Sales and payments are finalized with their contents at that time and are not recalculated if corrected later. Only expense entries (monthly) and advance payments received are totaled with the latest contents each time you open it. You can choose only days whose totals are finished.
  • Sales are excluding tax and count on the usage date (or the check-out date if there is none). Tax, charges not counted as sales and point use are not included, nor are the amounts of canceled reservations.
  • Payments that do not become receivables, such as cash, are counted on the payment date, and payments linked to an accounts receivable party (booking channel, card, etc.) are counted on the starting date set in the accounts receivable settings. The payment information CSV counts by payment date, so the amounts may not match even for the same period.
When numbers do not match between reportsEach report handles cancellations, points, tax and non-reservation items differently, and counts by different dates (usage date, payment date, starting date). First check When report numbers do not match.
