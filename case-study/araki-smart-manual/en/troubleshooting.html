When you hit an error or are unsure what to do

Troubleshooting

Do not re-enter things blindly. Go in this order: "keep the current state → check → contact the manager (person in charge) if needed."

What to do first

  1. Note the guest's name, the dates concerned, and the reservation number.
  2. Before dismissing a message on the screen, write down its wording or take a photo of the screen.
  3. Note what you pressed just before, and the operation right before that.
  4. Do not press the same button repeatedly; go to the steps for the symptom below.
Do notDo not force amounts to match, re-create the same reservation, rewrite an external reservation only in the PMS, or stop automatic integration without knowing why.

Reservation not found

  1. If you cannot find it on the room status chart, it may be a reservation with no room decided (unassigned). Unassigned reservations do not appear in the rooms on the status chart, so search in 予約管理 → 予約情報 (Reservation management → Reservation info) (from the official manual).
  2. Widen the date range by about one month before and after. By default, the reservation info list is narrowed to "check-in from today to one month ahead" (from the official manual).
  3. Search by name in kanji, in kana, and by family name only.
  4. Also search by phone number, reservation number, and lead guest name. Smart reservation search can also find companions' names and OTA reservation numbers. 「当日分の予約検索」 (Search today's reservations) on the status chart can search by group name, the name and furigana of the lead guest or guests, OTA reservation number, and room name (from the official manual).
  5. Also check canceled reservations, group reservations, and reservations for other properties. Canceled reservations do not appear unless you select 「キャンセルも表示する」 (Also show cancellations) in the filter (Search for a reservation).
  6. Check in the OTA's admin screen or the reservation notification that the booking was completed and for which property.
  7. If it exists externally but not in the PMS, do not register it twice; contact the manager.
If the guest is in front of you on the dayAsk to see the reservation confirmation email, and note the stay dates, number of guests, payment, and reservation number. Get the manager's decision before promising a vacant room.

Cannot save / an error appeared

  1. Check fields with a red frame or a 「必須」 (Required) mark.
  2. Check the order of dates, the number of guests against room capacity, and the phone number format.
  3. Check for error text at the top and bottom of the screen.
  4. Save only once and wait for a response.
  5. If it does not improve, record the error text, the time, the reservation concerned, and the steps you took.
  6. Before contacting, check on another screen that the reservation has not already been created or updated.
Before closing the browserRecord what you entered and the error. Logging in again may fix it, but if you close first, the cause and what you entered will be lost.

The reservation amount differs from the amount on screen

  1. Check the stay dates, number of nights, number of guests, and room type.
  2. Check additional charges, discounts, and tax and service charges.
  3. Check the booking channel and whether it is prepaid.
  4. Check the amounts in the change history and the reservation notification.
  5. If you cannot explain the reason for the difference, do not charge or refund; contact the manager.
For administrators: when a prepaid reservation shows 「未払い」 (Unpaid)The official manual names a possible cause: under 「設定」 (Settings) at the bottom left of the screen → 「宿泊予約サイト手数料設定」 (Booking site fee settings), ［現地払い］ (Pay at the hotel) may be checked for that payment method. The front desk does not charge twice and passes it to the manager, and the manager or administrator checks this setting (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

The OTA reservation differs from the PMS

Steps for when the stay dates, number of guests, room, charges, cancellation status, etc. differ.

  1. Check that the reservation number in the PMS and the one on the OTA or site controller side are the same.
  2. Check the last updated date and time on each.
  3. Note exactly which items differ.
  4. Do not correct only the PMS side; contact the manager.
  5. After checking where the change came from and the integration status, correct it on the screen you are told to use.
Do not stop automatic updates on your own judgmentAfter stopping, changes made externally are not reflected. Also, when it is restarted, what you corrected in the PMS may be reverted.
Main reasons OTA-side changes do not come in (from the official manual)
  • Changes that arrive while automatic updates are stopped are not imported, even if you restart later.
  • Some changes are not imported into reservations that are checked in or whose daily closing is done.
In either case, look at the OTA-side details and enter them by hand as the manager instructs (Stopping site controller automatic updates). Not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel.

How to undo (from the official manual)
  • Before daily closing: A wrong payment can be removed by opening the row's 「詳細」 (Details) in 《支払い情報》 (Payment info) of the reservation details and pressing 「削除する」 (Delete). For charges that are already settled, remove the payment first and then correct them (Registering charge information). A wrong check-in / check-out can be undone from the status at the top right of the reservation details with 「チェックインを取り消す」 (Undo check-in) or 「チェックアウトを取り消す」 (Undo check-out). At properties that allow check-in by room, you can undo it room by room (Check-in by room).
  • After daily closing: Charges and payments for a closed day can be neither changed nor deleted. Adjust them on a date that is not closed, or with 予約外_料金・精算 (Non-reservation charges and payments). The manager makes this adjustment (予約外_料金・精算 (Non-reservation charges and payments)).
Who may undo is awaiting confirmation on siteThe methods are as above, but who may undo a wrong check-in / check-out, cancel a payment, or issue a refund (permissions) will be decided after checking with HOTEL SMART and the Araki Hotel manager. Until then, record things as in the table above and contact the manager.

Emails do not reach the guest / cannot be sent

When a guest says an automatic email (reservation confirmation, check-in code, etc.) has not arrived, check the following in order (from the official manual; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

  1. Check that an email address is entered in the guest information in the reservation details. Emails cannot be sent for reservations from booking sites that do not pass on email addresses, such as Rakuten Travel and Ikyu (Automatic email delivery). Araki Hotel does not use SMS, so contact the guest by phone or through the booking site's messages.
  2. Check that the address is in a valid form. Emails cannot be sent to addresses with consecutive dots such as "..", a dot right before "@", or full-width characters mixed in.
  3. The send date and time for the automatic email may not have come yet. You can see when it was sent in ［付帯情報］ (Additional info) of the reservation details. Within one week, you can also see it in 「メール送信履歴」 (Email sending history) in the menu under the account name at the top right.
  4. If it was sent but has not arrived, possible causes are the spam folder, blocking on the receiving side, or delays on the receiving side. Ask the guest to check their spam folder.
  5. To resend by hand, use 「送信」 (Send) or 「再送」 (Resend) in ［付帯情報］ (Additional info) of the reservation details. These buttons send regardless of the automatic sending settings, so do not press them on test reservations.
  • The link in the email does not open: the receiving side may disable links, or the URL may be broken across lines. Tell the guest to copy the whole URL and paste it into the browser.
  • The sender is no-reply@hotelsmart.jp, which cannot receive replies. If the guest replies, it goes to the property's email address.
  • The body of sent emails cannot be viewed in the system. If you want a copy, have a BCC set for each email (administrator).
  • The check-in code email is sent at the set date and time even if the guest My Page entry is not finished. With the 「再送信を行う」 (Resend) setting, it is sent twice: when the entry is finished and at the set date and time.
  • A different text than the settings arrived: templates are separate for (未選択) (Not selected), Japanese, English, Chinese, and Korean. Which language is sent depends on the language the guest chose on the guest My Page or tablet, so fix all of them (administrator).
  • In Gmail, line breaks may not show. This cannot be fixed in the settings. Files can be attached to automatic emails.
  • To stop emails to guests, set automatic sending of each email to 「行わない」 (Do not send) in the automatic email delivery settings (administrator).

Numbers in reports or the dashboard do not match

Each report counts differently, so it is not unusual for numbers to differ. When you find a difference, first check whether it is explained by one of the differences below (from the official manual; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel). How to output reports is in Output reports and CSVs.

Report / screenDay countedIncluded / excluded
This month's actual sales on the dashboardUsage date (check-out date if none)Tax excluded. Includes non-reservation items, regardless of reservation status
Occupancy outputThe selected day (divided at the 04:00 closing time)Includes reservations canceled later. Excludes point use, tax, items not counted as sales, and partial payments
Payment info CSVPayment dateBoth reservations and non-reservation items. Refunds are deducted
Income and expenditure reportSales by usage date; payments by payment date or the account receivable's starting dateTax excluded. Does not include tax, items not counted as sales, points, or canceled reservations. Finalized after closing and does not change even if corrected later
Monthly totals in reports-Updated once a day. Check the totaling date and time at the top right
  • The closing time is 04:00, so payments between midnight and before 4:00 go into the previous day (Daily closing).
  • There is no report to check the breakdown of OTA prepayments by payment date.
  • To find out who changed charges or payments and when, check the 料金・精算_更新情報CSV (Charges and payments update info CSV).
  • For a difference whose reason you do not know, do not make amounts match; note the report name, period, and difference, and contact the manager.
