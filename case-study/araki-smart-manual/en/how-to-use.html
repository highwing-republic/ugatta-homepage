How to use this manual

This page explains what this manual can do, how to find things in it and how to read it. Read it once from top to bottom before you start.

What this manual can do

This manual is a "what to do when you are stuck" guide for using HOTEL SMART (the system that manages reservations, rooms and billing) at the Araki Hotel front desk.

  • Look up how to do a task: check the steps for check-in, payment, refunds, moving a guest to another room and more, with screen photos.
  • Search with whatever words come to mind: you can search with a word such as "refund" or with a sentence such as "move to another room".
  • Check the flow of the day: see what to check, and in what order, from the start of your shift until closing.
  • See what to do when something goes wrong: when a reservation cannot be found or an error appears, see what to check first.
  • Look up words you do not know: words such as "OTA" and "assign" are explained in plain language.
  • Print it and keep it at the desk: you can print a one-page quick reference with the daily checks and what to do in an emergency.
  • Find official manual articles: the same search also finds articles in the official manual published by the HOTEL SMART company, and opens them.
  • Read it in English: there is an English version. If you want to read it in Burmese, you can use your browser's translation feature.

What it cannot do, and things to be careful about

  • This manual is for reading only. You cannot change reservations or handle money here. All operations are done on the HOTEL SMART screen.
  • It is still a draft (work in progress). Some parts have not been checked on the real screens at Araki Hotel. Those parts say "not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel".
  • For prices, refunds and reservations from outside booking sites, do not change anything on a guess, even if it is written in the manual. If you are unsure, ask the manager (person in charge).
  • Card payments are taken on a separate card terminal that is not connected to HOTEL SMART. After taking the payment on the terminal, you also have to enter the payment in HOTEL SMART by hand.
If you are not sure, stop and call the managerIf you cannot find the answer in the manual, or the screen is different from what the manual says, do not force the operation. Contact the manager.

How to open the manual

If you are connected to the internet, you can open it on the front desk computer, a tablet or a smartphone.

  1. Open a browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.).
  2. Open the top page of this manual. The address is ugatta-llc.com/case-study/araki-smart-manual/ (the English version is in …/araki-smart-manual/en/).
  3. So that you can open it quickly next time, add it to your bookmarks (favorites). On a smartphone or tablet you can also use "Add to Home Screen".
To go back to the top pageOn any page, tap the "Araki Hotel Front Desk Quick Manual" text (logo) at the top left to return to the top page.

Finding things (1): search (the fastest way)

The search box is in the middle of the top page, and at the very top (header) of every page.

Type a word or a sentence

  1. Tap the search box and type what you want to look up.
  2. Suggestions appear below while you are typing.
  3. Tap the suggestion you want. You go straight to the heading of that step on that page.
Searching with a word
refund, receipt, daily closing, assign, check-out
Searching with a sentence
move to another room, how to cancel a reservation, reissue a receipt
You can also choose with a computer keyboardWhile suggestions are shown, choose with the ↓ ↑ keys and open with the Enter key. If you press Enter without choosing, the top suggestion opens. Press Esc to close the suggestions.

The yellow marks on the page you go to

When you go to a page from a suggestion, the words you searched for get a yellow mark (underline), so you can see right away where they are written. A bar at the top says it is highlighting the words that matched.

If the marks are in the way, press "Clear highlights" next to it. The marks are removed automatically when you print.

Seeing all the results

Press "See all results" (or "Open the results page" when there are only a few) at the bottom of the suggestions to see everything that was found as a list.

  • Press a button above the list (such as "Daily tasks" or "Troubleshooting") to show only the results from that page.
  • Each result shows a little of the text that was found. Read it and open the one that looks right.

Results marked "Official manual"

Results marked "Official manual (Japanese)" are articles in the official online manual published by the HOTEL SMART company. Pressing one opens the article on the official site in a new tab (in Japanese). This manual only lists the names of the official articles and links to them.

The official manual also covers other hotelsIt explains features that Araki Hotel does not use. When this manual has the same steps, look at this manual first.

If nothing is found

  • Try a shorter word (for example, "how do I give a refund" → "refund").
  • Try another way of saying it (for example, "void" → "cancel", "room allocation" → "assign").
  • If you still cannot find it, look at the Troubleshooting page or ask the manager.

Finding things (2): choose from the menu

Every page has a menu at the very top (on a smartphone, it runs sideways under the logo and you can swipe it left and right). The top page also has buttons for common tasks.

Using the top page

Today's front desk tasks
From the start of the shift to closing, in order from 1 to 7. Check them in order, starting at the top left.
Common problems
What to do when a reservation cannot be found, check-in does not work, or something cannot be saved.

How to read a page

Numbered steps

Steps with round numbers (1, 2, 3…) are done in order from the top. Do not skip steps. Items with a square (□) are a checklist for checking things.

Coloured boxes

Red box: stop and checkOperations that are hard to undo if done wrong, or things that need the manager's confirmation.
Yellow box: cautionCommon mistakes and things to be careful about.
Green box: OK when confirmedHow to tell that it was done correctly.
Blue-green box: tips and extra notesThings that are useful to know.

Marks inside the text

(from the official manual)
Checked in the official HOTEL SMART manual. It may be a little different from the Araki Hotel screens.
(not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel)
Not yet checked on the real screens at Araki Hotel. If the screen is different, tell the manager.
Japanese words in 「　」 or 【　】
The names of buttons and items shown on the HOTEL SMART screen, followed by the English in brackets. Look for the same Japanese text on the screen.

Screen photos and the table of contents

  • Photos marked "click to enlarge" are shown larger when you press them.
  • Long pages (Front desk operations, Initial setup) start with a table of contents. On a smartphone it is folded, so press it to open it.

Printing

  1. Open Quick reference in the menu.
  2. Press the print link at the bottom of the page.
  3. Choose the printer on the print screen and print. Keep it where you can see it at the front desk.

Other pages can also be printed with the browser's print (on a computer, hold the Ctrl key and press P). The menu and the search box are not printed.

Fill in the contacts on the quick referenceThe contacts for "the manager" and "HOTEL SMART support" on the quick reference are blank. Write them in by hand after printing.

Reading in English or Burmese

  • Press "English" in the header to switch to the English version of the page you are reading. On the English version, press 「日本語」 to go back.
  • On the English version, you can search with English words.
  • To read in Burmese, open the English version and translate it into Burmese with the translation feature of Chrome or Edge. The button names on the screen stay in Japanese, so you can compare them with the HOTEL SMART screen.

When you find a mistake or want something added

If the manual is different from the screen, hard to understand, or missing a step you need, tell the manager. Telling them the following helps get it fixed faster.

  1. Which page and which heading (the page name and the heading text)
  2. What is different, or what is hard to understand
  3. If possible, a photo of the HOTEL SMART screen
Do not photograph guest informationWhen you take a photo of the screen, make sure guest names and phone numbers are not in it.

Frequently asked questions

Can just reading this manual change anything in HOTEL SMART?
No. This manual is for reading only.
Which page should I read first?
First read Daily tasks, then print the Quick reference. For the other pages, it is fine to search and open them when you need them.
The search shows too many results and I do not know which one to open.
On the results list, press a page button to narrow them down. If you are unsure, start with the results from "Daily tasks" and "Reservations & billing".
Should I look at the official manual or this manual?
Look at this manual first. It is written for the way Araki Hotel works.
When is the content updated?
Whenever it is corrected. The date of the last update is at the very bottom of each page.
