Key points stated explicitly in the official PDF

  • As a rule, reservations made through the site controller are changed at the source where they were made
  • If you stop the automatic update of a reservation, later changes are not synced
  • Data that has gone through daily closing cannot be edited
  • Check the receipt PDF before printing it
  • Rooms cannot be blocked for periods that have reservations or are already closed (that blocks on closed dates cannot be changed or deleted is also stated in the online version. That rooms cannot be blocked for periods with reservations is stated only in the PDF, not in the online version, and has not been checked on the real screen either)

Questions for HOTEL SMART: room assignment

Items that could not be confirmed in the official PDF and online articles, or could not be verified without real data.

  1. Please tell us the official procedure for manually assigning a room from the unassigned list in 「部屋割り表」 (Room allocation chart). We would also like to confirm where to drag the schedule bar to and when it is saved.
    What the official articles tell us: apart from the room allocation chart, manual assignment can be done with ［部屋割り当て］ (Room assignment) in the reservation details (choosing a room number, or 「おまかせ割り当て」 (Auto-assign)) and with 「未部屋割りの予約を割り当てる」 (Assign unassigned reservations) on the room status chart (Room assignment 3.). Dragging is mentioned only in the article on arranged-item management, as an operation to "move an assigned reservation to another free room". → The question can be narrowed to "can a reservation be dragged from the unassigned list to a room?"
  2. Is there a way to stop a reservation that was manually unassigned with 「割当解除」 (Unassign) from being assigned again by the next automatic assignment? (That it is reassigned was confirmed on 2026-10-10.)
  3. How are room blocks and the check-in / check-out times of the preceding and following reservations taken into account when automatic assignment decides candidate rooms?
    What the official articles tell us: rooms that are blocked cannot be assigned and are removed from inventory (ブロック機能 article). The causes of automatic room assignment failure listed are no free room of the specified room type, and wrong check-in / check-out time settings on that reservation or the ones before and after it, causing the times to overlap into an overbooking (自動部屋割り設定 article). That it does not look at cleaning status was confirmed on 2026-10-09. → The official articles almost answer this.
  4. Please tell us the official correction procedure when a room type change arrives from an OTA or the site controller after a room has been assigned.
    What the official articles tell us: they only say "once a room is assigned, the change is not reflected" and recommend a setting that assigns rooms shortly before arrival. When the imported room type is wrong in the first place, they advise checking 「部屋タイプ割り当て」 (Room type mapping) in the site controller integration settings. → The question can be narrowed to "may we fix it by hand in the reservation details, and how does that relate to automatic updates?"
  5. Is there a screen where we can see the operation history of assignments, unassignments and changes, and who did them?
    What the official articles tell us: the only histories available are the operation history of charges and payment (料金・精算＿更新情報CSV (charges and payment update information CSV)) and the check-in / check-out, cancellation and stop / resume of automatic updates recorded automatically in the operator memo; there is no mention of a room assignment history. → Kept as a question.
  6. Floor bulk assignment can be saved even in an overbooked state; can the function be disabled or restricted by permission?
    What the official articles tell us: floor bulk assignment can be used only when 「フロア一括割当」 (Floor bulk assignment) is turned ON in 〖予約詳細表示設定〗 (Reservation details display settings) in the property details. It can be read that turning it OFF makes it unavailable. There is no mention of restrictions by permission. → Instead of asking, it is enough to check Araki Hotel's current setting (ON / OFF).
