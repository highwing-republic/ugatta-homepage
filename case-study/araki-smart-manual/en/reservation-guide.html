From reservation to receipt

Reservations, Changes and Billing

From finding a reservation to registering, changing and settling payment. For external reservations, the most important thing is to make changes in the right place.

Handled by: front deskMatches the official PDF
On this page1. Reservation search2. Individual reservations3. Group reservations4. Reservation changes5. Charges and payment6. Receipts

1. Search for a reservation

Left menu → 予約管理 (Reservation management) → 予約情報 (Reservation information)

First use 「スマート予約検索」 (Smart reservation search) at the top

  1. Enter one item you are sure of: name, furigana (phonetic reading), phone number, reservation number or check-in code.
  2. When candidates appear, also match one more item: stay date, number of guests or phone number.
  3. Open 「詳細」 (Details) for the correct reservation.
Checkboxes next to the search box (from the official manual)By default, Smart reservation search only looks for reservations with check-out today or later. To find past reservations, tick 「過去分も表示」 (Show past ones too) next to the search box. To narrow to reservations leaving today, tick 「本日チェックアウト」 (Check-out today). For the match method, 「前方一致」 (Starts with) is recommended (with 「部分一致」 (Contains), for example, searching 「山田」 (Yamada) also brings up 「山本」 (Yamamoto) and 「田中」 (Tanaka)).
What Smart reservation search can find (from the official manual)Besides name, phone number and check-in code, you can also search by booking channel, rate plan, room type, room number, corporate/group name and company name. The text of guest requests, operator memos, repeat memos and cleaning notes is also searched. Names and phone numbers are searched with hyphens, spaces and commas removed. Uppercase and lowercase letters, and hiragana and katakana, are not distinguished. You can also search with the keyword left empty (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).

To narrow down from the list

  1. Open 「条件を選択して絞り込み」 (Select conditions to filter).
  2. Set the check-in and check-out dates a little wider than the period you are looking for.
  3. Only if needed, also choose conditions such as cancellation status, room assignment status and occupancy status (「過去分も表示」 (Show past ones too) and 「本日チェックアウト」 (Check-out today) are not in the filter panel; they are next to Smart reservation search at the top).
  4. Change the sort order to one that fits your purpose, such as 「チェックイン（昇順）」 (Check-in, ascending).
  5. Match the representative's name, check-in code, stay dates, room name, booking channel and stay status.
What was checked on the real screenThe reservation list shows: property name, representative's name, check-in code, in/out, number of nights, room name, memo, booking channel, stay status, last updated, and details.
Conditions you can filter by (from the official manual)
  • Besides check-in and check-out, you can filter by reservation received date (including the date imported from the site controller) and reservation updated date.
  • You can also filter by occupancy status (provisional / not checked in / staying / checked out), repeat status and overbooking.
  • For cancellations, you can choose 「キャンセルも表示する」 (Also show cancellations) or 「キャンセルのみ表示する」 (Show cancellations only).
  • Name, check-in code, phone number, email address, company name, representative's name and group name are searched as "starts with"; OTA reservation numbers are searched as "contains".
  • The default sort order is earliest check-in date first. The conditions shown at first (check-in from today to one month ahead) cannot be changed.
  • To search by room number, use Smart reservation search at the top.

2. Register an individual reservation

予約管理 (Reservation management) → 予約情報 (Reservation information) → 新規予約 (New reservation)
  1. Check that 「物件名」 (Property name) is correct (Araki Hotel has two properties: 「種子島あらきホテル」 (Tanegashima Araki Hotel) and 「種子島あらき別館」 (Tanegashima Araki Annex)).
  2. Enter the stay date and number of nights.
  3. Choose the number of adults and children, the room type and the number of rooms.
  4. Enter the guest's name, furigana (phonetic reading) and phone number.
  5. Check the rate plan, charges and payment method.
  6. If there are special notes, enter them in a short sentence anyone can understand.
  7. Read through the contents and press 「保存」 (Save) only once.
Forgetting or choosing the wrong property nameAccording to the official manual, you cannot save unless you choose a property name (the save button does not appear). Also watch out for choosing the wrong property, such as creating an annex reservation under the main building.
Unconfirmed reservations are 「仮予約」 (provisional reservations) (from the official manual)A reservation that is not yet confirmed can be registered by ticking 「仮予約にする」 (Make provisional). While it is provisional, automatic emails are not sent and it is not counted in some reports. Untick it once confirmed (see Front operations for details; not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
Prevent duplicate registrationEven if the response is slow after saving, do not press repeatedly. Check in the reservation list whether it was registered before trying again.

For detailed steps, see Front operations (detailed steps): 2. Creating a reservation (individual reservation)

3. Register a group reservation

予約管理 (Reservation management) → 予約情報 (Reservation information) → 新規予約 (New reservation) → 宿泊変更 (Change stay)

There is no separate entry point for group reservations. In the same 「新規予約」 (New reservation) screen as individual reservations, set multiple rooms and multiple guests.

  1. In 「代表者情報」 (Representative information), enter the group representative's name and contact details.
  2. In 「予約情報」 (Reservation information), enter the basic conditions such as stay dates and rate plan.
  3. In 「人数変更」 (Change number of guests) under 「宿泊変更」 (Change stay), set the number of rooms per room type and the number of guests in each room.
  4. In 「金額変更」 (Change amount), check the charges per room and per day, and change them only if needed, following the rules of the manager (person in charge).
  5. If you decide room numbers at the time of reservation, assign them in 「部屋変更」 (Change room).
  6. In 「宿泊者情報」 (Guest information), check which guest is in which room.
  7. Record who pays, the billing address and any undecided items in 「共有事項」 (Shared notes), check the booking channel and the reservation received date and time, and save.
Difference from an individual reservationThe entry point and screen are the same. The difference is that you set multiple rooms, guests, charges and room numbers in 「宿泊変更」 (Change stay). Do not finish after registering only the representative.
Read it back before savingCheck the whole group's stay dates, number of rooms, number of guests and charges, and the guest assignment for each room.

For detailed steps, see Front operations (detailed steps): 3. Creating a reservation (group reservation)

4. Change a reservation

  1. Check 「予約経路」 (Booking channel) in the reservation details.
  2. For phone or direct reservations, check the change and the difference in charges, and edit it in the PMS.
  3. For reservations via an OTA or travel agency, as a rule, change it at the source and wait for the sync.
  4. Read back the dates, number of guests, room and charges after the change.
  5. If the room or dates changed, share it with the cleaning staff and the next shift.
Do not change external reservations only in the PMSAccording to the official PDF, reservations via the site controller are, as a rule, changed on the booking site side. If you change only the PMS, a later automatic update may revert it, and inventory or charges may get out of sync. According to the official manual, for imported reservations the date/time, rate plan, number of rooms, number of guests, charges and payment cannot be changed from the admin screen.
Stopping automatic updates is the manager's decisionIf you stop automatic updates for a reservation, later changes are no longer synced. If you cannot confirm the reason, the target reservation and the conditions for lifting it, do not do it.
Inventory is sent automatically from HOTEL SMART to TL-LincolnAccording to the official manual, when you change dates, inventory must be adjusted on the site controller side unless the connection with the site controller is "2way". Araki Hotel uses two-way integration with TL-Lincoln, so when you change dates or the number of rooms in the PMS, cancel a reservation, or block a room, inventory is sent automatically from HOTEL SMART's 【販売在庫】 (Sales inventory) to TL-Lincoln (it can take up to 15 minutes to be reflected). Even if you fix remaining rooms by hand on the TL-Lincoln screen, it will be overwritten, so the manager adjusts inventory on the HOTEL SMART side (from the official manual; see Front operations for details, and for settings, Initial setup: "Sales inventory").

For detailed steps, see Front operations (detailed steps): 4. Changing a reservation (by pattern)

5. Register charges and take payment

Reservation details → 料金・精算情報 (Charges and payment information)
  1. Check that the room charge, extra charges and discounts are correct.
  2. Check the payment terms, such as prepaid, pay at the hotel or invoice.
  3. Enter the amount received and the payment method.
  4. Check that the outstanding balance is 0 yen, or that the billing address is correct.
  5. Match the total on screen against the actual cash and card slips.
What looks differentWhat to check
Higher or lower than the reservation confirmationCheck the number of guests, number of nights, extra charges, discounts and tax
Shows unpaid even though it is prepaidCheck the booking channel and payment information, and do not charge twice
Cash does not matchDo not change amounts to make it match; check the transaction history and the actual cash with the manager
Cancellations and refunds after payment go to the managerUntil the correct way to fix it is confirmed, do not add an opposite amount or move it to another payment method on your own judgment.

For detailed steps, see Front operations (detailed steps): 5. Registering charge information (sales) / 6. Registering payment information

Rooms with an outstanding balance can be listed in 【売上管理】 (Sales management) → 【滞在中精算チェックリスト】 (In-house payment checklist) (for details, see Front operations (detailed steps)).

For payment using a corporate or group advance payment (「前受金の取り崩し」 (Draw down advance payment)), payment billed later (「後日決済」 (Pay later)), and registering receipts of accounts receivable, see Front operations: "Corporate/group advance payments and receivables (pay later)" (from the official manual).

6. Issue a receipt

Reservation details → 利用明細・領収書 (Statement and receipt)
  1. Check the receipt amount and that it has been paid. Create it after registering the payment (according to the official manual, the purpose line can only be entered when there is a receipt amount; if you create it before payment, it becomes a statement only).
  2. Confirm the addressee and the purpose line with the guest.
  3. Issue the receipt and open the created PDF.
  4. Check the date, addressee, amount and purpose line, then print.
Do not issue for amounts prepaid on the booking siteAccording to the official manual, HOTEL SMART issues receipts for amounts paid at the hotel, and for amounts prepaid on the booking site, the guest gets the receipt from the booking site. To prevent duplicate issuing, direct guests to the booking site's receipt for prepaid amounts (use 「領収項目に絞る」 (Limit to receipt items) as needed).
Explanation in the official PDFDownload the receipt as a PDF and print it after checking the contents. When issuing a second time, follow the property's rules so it is clear that it is a reissue.
How to know it's doneThe payment amount on screen, the receipt amount and the amount actually received all match.
Guests who issue receipts on My Page (from the official manual)Araki Hotel uses the guest My Page. If receipt issuing on My Page is enabled, guests can issue a receipt themselves from My Page after check-out, entering the addressee. To avoid the front desk issuing the same receipt again, ask the guest whether they have issued one (the receipt issuing setting is not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel; see Front operations for details).
