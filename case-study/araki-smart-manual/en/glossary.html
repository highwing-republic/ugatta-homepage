Plain-language glossary

Explains words often used in HOTEL SMART and hotel work, as far as needed for operating the system.

Reservations and sales

PMS
A system that manages a hotel's reservations, rooms, billing and more in one place. Here it means HOTEL SMART.
OTA
An online travel booking site (online travel agency). As a rule, reservations made through an external site are changed at the site where they were made.
サイトコントローラー (site controller)
A system that connects inventory and reservation data between several booking sites and the PMS. Stopping the integration is the manager's decision. Araki Hotel uses TL-Lincoln.
2way連携 (two-way integration)
An integration that not only imports reservations from the site controller (1way) but also sends the PMS inventory back to the site controller. Araki Hotel has a two-way integration with TL-Lincoln (owner's decision, 2026-10-11). According to the official manual, inventory is managed as HOTEL SMART's sales inventory (rooms − reservations − blocks + adjustment), and any change is sent to TL-Lincoln automatically (it can take up to about 15 minutes to be reflected). Reservations and blocks created in the PMS are also reflected in TL-Lincoln's inventory (not yet confirmed at Araki Hotel).
在庫 (inventory)
The number of rooms that can accept reservations. Both the rooms actually free and the sales settings affect it.
プラン (rate plan)
A product for sale that combines charges, meals, cancellation terms and so on.
予約経路 (booking channel)
The entry point through which the reservation came in, such as phone, the hotel's own website, an OTA or a travel agency.

Stays and rooms

アサイン／部屋割り (room assignment)
Assigning an actual room number to a reservation.
チェックイン (check-in)
The process that puts an arriving guest into the in-house (staying) status.
チェックインコード (check-in code)
A 6-digit number for each reservation. The guest receives it by email or on My Page and enters it on the check-in tablet to check in.
セルフチェックイン（タブレット） (tablet self check-in)
The guest enters guest information and completes ID verification on a tablet to check in. Araki Hotel uses it. Tablet self check-in
サポート通話 (support call)
A feature that lets the guest make a video call from the tablet to the property's staff. It is separate from the paid call center (ID verification only).
チェックアウト (check-out)
The process that checks charges and keys and sets the reservation to checked out.
ノーショー (no-show)
The guest has a reservation but does not arrive and does not contact the hotel. If it is to be treated as a cancellation, the front desk can do it, but always report to the manager (person in charge) after you do. Charging no-show fees or refunding is the manager's decision. The official manual advises cancelling as soon as you know the guest will not come (before daily closing), because a reservation left as a no-show is counted in sales and occupancy. On the room status chart it is shown as 「ノーショウ」 (No-show) (a reservation that started on a previous day and has not checked in yet).
客室ブロック (room block)
Making a room unavailable for reservations and assignment because of a fault, construction work and so on.
連泊 (multi-night stay)
The same guest staying 2 or more nights in a row.
未部屋割り (unassigned)
A reservation whose room type is decided but whose room number is not yet decided. 「未部屋割り」 (Unassigned) on the dashboard counts only reservations staying tonight (from the official manual). It does not appear on rooms in the room status chart. Assign it in Room assignment.
自動部屋割り失敗 (automatic room assignment failed)
A reservation that automatic room assignment could not put into a room. It happens, for example, when no room of the same type is free. You can find them from the number on the dashboard.
おまかせ割り当て (auto-assign)
A button in the room assignment area of the reservation details. The system picks a room and assigns it according to the automatic room assignment settings.
オーバーブッキング (overbooking)
Two or more reservations with overlapping dates are in the same room. Do not move them on your own; contact the manager.
ルームチェンジ (room change)
A feature that swaps an assigned room for another room. The system suggests candidate rooms.
宿泊変更 (change stay)
The screen in the reservation details for changing the number of guests and rooms (change number of guests), the room for each day (change room) and the charges (change amount).
清掃待ち／清掃中／確認待ち／清掃済 (waiting for cleaning / cleaning / waiting for check / cleaned)
Cleaning statuses in the cleaning schedule. Waiting for cleaning means it has not started yet, cleaning means work is in progress, waiting for check means the cleaner's report is waiting to be checked, and cleaned means it is finished.
手配（未手配／手配済） (arrangement: not arranged / arranged)
Whether a cleaning company or cleaner has been assigned to the cleaning schedule. Araki Hotel has not registered any cleaners.
共有事項 (shared notes)
The memo fields in the reservation details. They include the operator memo (for staff; operations such as check-in / check-out are also recorded automatically) and cleaning remarks (for cleaning; not shown on the printed making list).

Charges and operations

事前決済 (prepaid)
Already paid before arrival, on a booking site or elsewhere. Check the payment information so you do not charge twice.
現地決済 (pay at the hotel)
Receiving payment at the hotel.
未収金 (outstanding balance)
An amount not yet received. Check that it is 0 yen or that the correct billing party is set.
料金／精算 (charges / payment)
「料金」 (charges) is the amount billed to the guest; 「精算」 (payment) is the amount actually received (from the official manual).
前受金 (advance payment)
Money received before the stay or use. You can see the balance in the report 「前受金・滞在未収金残高一覧」 (Advance payment and in-house receivable balance list).
滞在未収金 (in-house receivable)
An amount not yet received from a guest who is staying (checked in). In-house receivable CSV
稼働率 (occupancy rate)
The number of rooms sold divided by the number of rooms. It is shown in the middle of the dashboard.
日次締め (daily closing)
The process that finalizes the sales and payments for the business day. After closing, they cannot be edited. At properties where it is enabled, the 「日次の締めを行う」 (Do daily closing) button appears after the set closing time, and the manager checks reservations and payments before pressing it to close the day.
マイページ (guest My Page)
A dedicated page where guests check their reservation and enter information. Araki Hotel uses it (it does not use SMS or LINE notices). If there is a problem, see Problems with My Page.
オプション (options)
Services and items added to a stay. Pick-up, rental items, extra dishes and so on; the contents differ depending on the property settings.
