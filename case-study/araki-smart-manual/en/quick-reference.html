Front desk quick reference

If unsure, check in this order. For detailed steps, open each manual.

5 checks at the start of your shift

  • Handover from the previous shift
  • Today's arrivals, departures and multi-night stays
  • Unassigned reservations (「未部屋割り」 (Unassigned) on the dashboard counts only tonight's stays. Check tomorrow and later too)
  • Check unpaid and prepaid reservations
  • Rooms that are uncleaned, out of order or blocked

Checks so you don't mix up guests

  1. Ask the name
  2. Match the stay date
  3. Match the phone number or number of guests
  4. Check room, charges and booking channel before operating

When to stop operating right away

  • You can't find the reservation
  • The OTA reservation details differ from the PMS
  • An outstanding balance is shown even though the reservation is prepaid
  • You can't explain the reason for a difference in charges
  • Only uncleaned or blocked rooms are left
  • You are asked for daily closing, a refund or stopping the integration
Do not click the same button repeatedly. Do not recreate the reservationWrite down the message on the screen and the last thing you did, and contact the manager (person in charge).

When to report to the manager after operating

  • You cancelled a reservation → report to the manager

What to tell the manager

① Time it happened　② Guest name / reservation number　③ Screen you have open　④ What you did just before　⑤ Message shown　⑥ Whether the guest is waiting
